# Spill911: business profile

_Facts only, each with source and date. Currency not stated on most calls; confirm AUD vs USD (see open-questions). Calls cover 2025-04-21 to 2026-11-11. Where the same call also covered Everything Truck Parts, only Spill facts are kept here._

## What the business is
- Shopify store selling spill containment, absorbents, material handling, safety and storage products to businesses and government (calls 2025-04-21, 2025-06-27). Mainly large orders to government and industry; high average order value (Bill, call 2025-06-27).
- Pesticide storage cabinets are sold to businesses for flammable storage; the store does not sell pest control (call 2025-04-21).
- Revenue includes government and reorder sales not on Shopify (call 2025-04-21). Offline transactions are close to double online volume, and high-value orders often happen outside Shopify (call 2025-08-06). Offline sales are tracked outside Shopify and are not in analytics (call 2025-10-29).
- Typical order a couple of hundred dollars; big-ticket orders average several thousand (John, call 2026-07-10; not clearly Spill-only).
- Government season matters: quoting picks up in season (call 2025-05-15); offline reorders rose during a government slowdown (call 2025-10-29); Hiver kept until the end of the government season, HubSpot after it (call 2026-08-19).
- GSA contract status and an authorised Vestil dealership are trust points for pages (call 2026-10-30). GSA Advantage is the main federal procurement portal for government buyers; competitors bid on "GSA approved" keywords (call 2026-04-01).
- Government leads: about 100 a day reviewed by John for viable bids; sources SAM.gov, E-Buy, distributor emails, award-history databases (call 2026-10-28).

## Customers and buying behaviour
- Customers often search by part number or specific need; some products are niche (calls 2025-08-06, 2025-09-03). B2B buying often has one person who looks and another who buys (call 2025-08-06).
- Large orders can take many touches (for example 15 sessions over 28 days, call 2025-08-20); one large lead went from first contact 30 April to order 21 July (call 2026-07-22).
- Shipping cost is the suspected main conversion barrier; shipping often exceeds product price (calls 2026-03-17, 2026-05-27). Of 5 abandoners contacted, 3 bought elsewhere citing shipping (call 2026-05-27).
- Contact Us form is the primary lead source; most contact-form leads appear to be third-party bidders or intermediaries, no confirmed purchases tied to them (calls 2026-09-16, 2026-08-05).
- ChatGPT is a notable referral source for large online orders (calls 2025-07-09, 2026-08-19, 2026-10-14); first Claude-attributed conversion seen (call 2026-11-11).
