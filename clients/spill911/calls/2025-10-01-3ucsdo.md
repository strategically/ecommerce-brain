## Decisions
- Use a three-tier attribute structure: company (core SKU, MPN, product metadata), business (Spill 911 operations and brands), and category (spill containment, absorbents, material handling) (call 2025-10-01).
- Start the attribute rollout with the spill containment category (call 2025-10-01).
- Spill containment attributes to capture: capacity and size (absorbent capacity, sump or container capacity, length, width, height, weight); materials and resistances (primary material, chemical, temperature, UV and rust resistance); features and handling (containment type, portability, foldable, fork pockets, drain, stackable); compliance and identification (EPA, OSHA and FM certifications, colour, origin, warranty) (call 2025-10-01).
- Material-handling attributes to capture: handling type, load capacity, lift height, platform length; primary material, wheel type, diameter and count, swivel and brakes; stacking, folding, corrosion resistance, temperature rating, warranty (call 2025-10-01).
- Extraction method: AI reads titles, images, vendor pages and part numbers (call 2025-10-01).
