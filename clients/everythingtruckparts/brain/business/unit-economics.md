# Everything Truck Parts: unit economics

_Margins, fees, break-even. Source and date on every line. Figures are as stated on calls; none are computed. Conflicting figures are in `open-questions.md`._

## Margins
- Filters are the highest-margin products; Sam said filter margin was 35% (call 2025-06-30).
- John sees about $35 profit per filter sale (call 2025-04-29).
- Sam estimates Fleet Guard, Donaldson and Wix can run at about 30-35% gross margin (call 2026-02-17).
- Fleet Guard has the largest margin, partly because sales tax charged on purchases is marked up into pricing (call 2026-02-17).
- John does not think margins allow a 10% sitewide discount such as the one Summit Racing offers (call 2025-04-29).
- Competitor Geno's Garage prices aggressively, pressuring margin and conversion (call 2025-07-07).

## Discounts and pricing
- Filter offer discussed: 10% off when a customer buys 12 or more of the same filter (call 2025-04-29).
- Tiered quantity discounts apply to Wix, Donaldson, Baldwin and Fleet Guard, handled by a third-party app (call 2025-07-22). Existing tiers are 1 to 5, 6 to 11 and 12+, and can be reused for kits (call 2026-09-29).
- Kit pricing: no discount decided; kit price currently equals the sum of the filters (call 2026-10-27). This replaces the sum-minus-10% example (call 2026-09-29).

## Costs that erode margin
- High shipping cost relative to price, especially on low-cost filters, is a frequent complaint and a likely abandonment driver (call 2025-07-07).
- Shipping estimates understate real freight for large truck-only items (call 2026-03-26).
- About 2,300 placeholder weights overstate checkout shipping, including 2,019 WIX items (call 2026-11-10).
- Drop-ship fees make low-price SKUs uneconomic to fulfil (call 2026-03-03).
- Returns and shipping costs from Turn14, Fleet Guard and a third-party warehouse reduce net margins (call 2026-01-19).
- Returns were about 25% of sales (call 2026-06-09). Estimated cancellations of about 35% reduce effective ROAS (call 2026-09-29).
- Convermax fee of about $250 per month (call 2026-03-26).
- Agency fee: about $2,500 per month (call 2026-01-19); $1,500 per month for three months from 2026-02-17 (call 2026-02-17).
- Turn 14 stock-held-for-ETP option needs up-front purchase that ETP cannot currently fund (call 2026-02-17).
