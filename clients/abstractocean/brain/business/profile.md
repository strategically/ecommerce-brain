_Facts only, each with source and date. Calls cover 2025-04-17 to 2026-11-09 (97 notes). ## What the business is
- Shopify Plus store selling aftermarket accessories for Tesla (Cybertruck, Model 3, Model Y, Model S and X) and Rivian vehicles; homepage message "the world's number one aftermarket Tesla accessory store" (call 2025-05-14).
- Store moved from BigCommerce to Shopify in mid-to-late 2021 (call 2025-05-14).
- Hero product groups: armour / paint protection film (PPF), lighting, and screen protectors (call 2025-12-19). Armour is sold under the Expel brand name (names vary in notes, see open-questions) (calls 2025-12-15, 2026-05-27).
- Other lines: emblems and badges, vinyl decals, camera covers, phone charging, air filters (recurring product), floor mats, door seals, wheel trims, centre-console items (calls 2025-07-16, 2026-03-02, 2026-05-27, 2025-05-16).
- Some products are sold on behalf of partners (call 2025-07-16). Collective (partner) products carry about 30 to 35% margin (call 2026-09-28).
- Rivian emblems bring about $40k a year of revenue sold off the main site, with takedown notices received; the process is manual (contact form, support form, invoice) (call 2025-12-19). Rivian emblems are sold off-site (call 2025-07-21).
- Model S and X together are under 3% of Tesla sales and about 2% of the addressable market; de-emphasised (calls 2025-05-12, 2025-12-19).
- About 95 to 99% of orders are US-based (call 2026-06-29). No Canada paid campaigns because of tariffs, duties and carrier handling fees (call 2026-09-21).
- Shopify feeds hold about 1,175 USA products, 227 Canadian and 163 French-Canadian products (call 2026-11-09). About 280 parent products (call 2026-02-23).
- About 100 to 120 legacy SKUs retired, with about 100 already retired during 2025 (call 2025-12-19).

## Customers and buying behaviour
- Returning-customer rate has ranged from about 25% to 60% across calls depending on day and period: 60% (call 2025-04-25); about 28 to 31% (call 2025-12-15); about 40 to 45% (call 2025-12-17). Not comparable (see open-questions).
- Customers typically need about five to eight sessions before purchase (call 2025-08-15); three or four journeys (call 2025-09-15). High-value armour buyers may take about 30 days and multiple visits (call 2025-08-20).
- Armour customers often buy a smaller first item before a larger kit; gateway products not yet identified (call 2026-10-05). About 40% of sales include some discount, and average discounting has fallen over 12 months (call 2026-10-05).
- Cybertruck owners are thought to be close to saturated as an email audience (calls 2025-07-21, 2025-07-23, 2025-08-15). Cybertruck buyers estimated at about 2,000 per quarter (call 2026-01-12); a third-party estimate of about 5,000 Cybertrucks shipped per quarter (call 2025-10-17).
- Tesla and Rivian still account for 90%+ of sales for a peer seller; other vehicle markets are weak (call 2026-01-12).
- Model Y and Model 3 have much larger addressable markets than Cybertruck or Model S and X (call 2025-07-14). Model 3 is about 95% of sales for the PPF supplier (call 2026-02-23).
- Customers wrongly ordered the Model Y screen protector size after a manufacturer size change, causing returns; a metafield popup now makes customers confirm screen size (call 2026-04-06).
- Pete says the Tesla-enthusiast audience tolerates a clumsier site (call 2025-05-14).
- ChatGPT-attributed orders are visible and converting; ChatGPT is one of the highest-converting non-search channels after DuckDuckGo (call 2026-05-06). Claude is unlikely to be a revenue channel (call 2026-05-11).
- A YouTube influencer video drove a significant share of one week's revenue and new customers, with conversion rate about 7%; judged non-repeatable; most inbound influencer requests are low quality (calls 2026-09-21, 2026-09-28).
