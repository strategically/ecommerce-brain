# VIP 360: unit economics

_Margins, fees, break-even. Source and date on every line._

- Target profit margin 40% on acquiring clients and 80 to 85% on banking clients (call 2026-10-02).
- Acquiring: weekly settlement, fees set by risk profile, VIP 360 handles chargebacks and fraud monitoring (call 2026-10-02).
- Pricing is above market averages, aimed at high-net-worth, high-volume clients (call 2026-10-02).
- Merchants are assessed on total volume more than ticket size; medium to high tickets (£50 to £100) limit chargeback risk. The two statements are not reconciled (call 2026-10-02).
- Medium and high-risk clients carry higher fees, which is the commercial reason for the bundle of high-ticket goods with bank accounts and cards (call 2026-09-17).
- No retainer fee, cost per lead or customer value stated.
