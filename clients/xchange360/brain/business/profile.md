# Xchange360: business profile

_Facts only, each with source and date. Calls cover 2026-06-10 to 2026-11-06 (10 notes, 1 failed recording). Engagement is an SEO, content and link-building retainer plus Google Ads and landing pages from late October 2026. Currency is stated only on the customer-value figure; see open-questions._

## What the business is
- Xchange360 is a B2B crypto platform for businesses that never want to touch crypto: accept crypto payments, convert to fiat, regulated enterprise rails (call 2026-07-08). It sits in the BLK Group, which also owns Epayclub, a high-risk payment processing brand (calls 2026-06-10, 2026-09-24).
- Core sellable products: digital asset exchange and OTC, payment processing and merchant services with payouts, custodian treasury and crypto cards, and a pay-by-crypto / pay-by-invoice gateway (call 2026-06-10). Other offerings: white-label crypto platforms, private custody, liquidity and market-making, high-risk banking (call 2026-06-10).
- Pay-by-invoice / pay-by-crypto is the primary wedge product for sales (call 2026-06-10). Positioning: one regulated desk for the full flow (bank, exchange, payouts), emphasising regulated rails and stability over consumer off-ramp pricing (call 2026-06-26).
- Gaming revenue exists but is kept off the main pages to protect the regulatory position (call 2026-07-08). The card product is not for public disclosure on the site yet (call 2026-07-15).
- Target customers: large enterprise customers processing high-volume crypto flow; about 50 mid-size customers preferred over 1,000 low-margin ones (calls 2026-06-26, 2026-07-08). Buyer contacts are often the CFO, payroll or accountants; buyer crypto knowledge varies widely (call 2026-10-22).
- Early-win verticals: marine, auto, real estate, luxury and jewellery, gaming, high-value services (call 2026-06-10); content verticals named include auto, real estate and yachts (call 2026-07-08).
- Sales: direct relationships, introducing brokers and partner networks; enterprise deals prioritised over pure organic (call 2026-06-10). Sales capacity is James Hickson plus two reps; a CRM (Attio) and outreach tool (Dench) were being set up (calls 2026-06-10, 2026-06-26).
- No standardised pricing existed as of 2026-06-10; pricing varies per client and is not published on the site (calls 2026-06-10, 2026-07-15).

## Regulatory and sales constraints
- Solicitation in Europe is blocked until a MiCA licence is obtained; inbound European customers can be accepted; outbound is allowed to Africa and Vancouver (call 2026-06-26). A second MiCA application is planned (call 2026-09-24). Whether Europe content is pushed depends on the licence timing (call 2026-09-24).
- Landing pages claiming crypto acceptance need compliance and legal sign-off on disclaimers and claims (call 2026-11-06).
