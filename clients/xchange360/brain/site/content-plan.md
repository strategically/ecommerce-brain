## Positioning rules (plan 2026-08-20)
- Lead promise: customer pays in crypto, client is paid in fiat, never touches crypto. B2B only. Spine is one regulated desk for the whole flow of funds, not an off-ramp tool.
- Compare against SWIFT on speed and cost only. No rate cards or pricing strategy (James).
- No fabricated case studies or named customers; vertical narratives only.
- Gaming is never the lead message and stays off main nav and sitemap (regulator-halo risk during CASP/MiCA licensing).
- High-risk / MCC / high-risk merchant content is out of scope for Xchange360 and belongs to the VIP 360 plan, but a refresh of the existing MCC explainer (R3) was approved on the 2026-11-06 call (see open question below).
- No EU or UK solicitation language until MiCA / UK permissions are confirmed by Matthias.
- Check cannibalisation before every piece in crowded clusters (stablecoin settlement, invoicing, real estate, OTC, custody, generic gateway).
