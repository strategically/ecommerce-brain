# Balloonery: unit economics

_Margins, fees, break-even. Source and date on every line. Period performance (revenue, spend, orders by city) is not recorded here; it stays in the call notes and database. Currency-less dollar amounts and unit-unclear ROAS figures are in open-questions.md._

## Cost structure
- COGS about 20-25% (call 2026-11-19).
- Cost lines: inventory and supplies, production labour, delivery and vehicle costs, rent; customer service treated as a fixed cost line and its hours have been cut (call 2026-07-30). Labour and delivery are the largest cost lines; hours already cut and further savings slower (call 2026-08-25). Labour and fixed-cost monthly amounts are in open-questions (call 2026-11-19).
- Toronto carries a high fixed-cost base relative to its margin (call 2026-08-25).
- Large inventory stocked, slow to convert to cash; supplier restocking fees apply to returns (call 2026-07-30). Payment plan with the inventory supplier to be asked (call 2026-07-30).
- Cash position described as tight, with bills coming due (Shawn, call 2026-08-25). Toronto is the main cash priority (call 2026-09-10).
- Partners paid at month end; Shopify payouts about seven days after orders (calls 2026-03-12, 2026-09-10).
- Delivery fees shown at checkout are the main friction in new markets (call 2026-09-10). Mid-funnel losses attributed to shipping and checkout friction (call 2026-07-02).
- Draft orders are higher-ticket and more margin-rich (call 2026-03-12).

## Partner economics
- Models considered: higher commission of 35 to 45 percent with minimal startup inventory, or partner-funded inventory with Balloonery capturing wholesale margin (call 2026-07-02).
- US model options: fee increase to around 35 percent, fixed monthly partner fee or subscription, decoupled customer price and partner payout for corporate orders, outbound corporate sales (call 2026-09-10). None adopted.
- Referral payments to partners are not paid initially (call 2026-07-02). Partners requested higher product prices (call 2026-02-12).
- A fee-based service model for Houston was discussed, to be modelled after Houston results (call 2026-08-12).

## Pricing tests
- Price increase tested in Canada on online sales only; test runs to 20 orders before a decision; offline prices to be matched if adopted (call 2026-08-25). Result not yet recorded.
