# Shokay: business profile

_Facts only, each with source and date. Current state as of the last call with business content (2026-02-05)._

## What the business is
- Describes itself as a global leader in sustainable yak wool (call 2025-05-29).
- Supply chain: fibre bought from herders, hand combing, sorting by colour and quality, washing, carding, spinning, knitting; 1% of each sale goes to local communities (call 2025-05-29).
- Says it is the first company to certify sustainable yak wool and helped set the animal welfare standards; holds one yak certification (call 2025-05-29). Textile Exchange is named as the key certification body; SFA set the cashmere and yak standard (call 2025-05-29).
- Described as preferred fibre supplier to a major fashion retailer; yak wool was used in a flagship coffee-chain store interior. The note attributes this to Carol personally (call 2025-05-29).
- Inventory is held in China, shipping around seven days (call 2025-08-07).
- Direct-to-consumer sales run through a China storefront, not the Shopify store (call 2025-08-07). Chinese buyers mainly purchase on local platforms (Taobao, RED, local TikTok), driven by live shopping and KOL activity (call 2025-10-02).
- Most orders are reported as coming from the US; men's products perform well and men's buying is repeat-purchase (call 2025-09-18).

## Audience
- Priority is higher-quality wholesale leads, aimed at smaller designer brands and boutiques; large brands are a separate audience (call 2025-07-24).
- Low-quality leads: people wanting to sell to Shokay and enquiries about non-core products such as cotton yarn and bamboo (call 2025-07-24).
- B2C focus markets discussed: cold, urban, affluent regions including Vancouver, Seattle, New York and Nordic countries (call 2025-08-07).
- Positioning options discussed: "more affordable than cashmere", "more exclusive than cashmere", "natural luxury" (call 2025-09-18). No choice recorded.
- Cashmere (and possibly alpaca) keywords prioritised over generic wool (call 2025-09-18).
